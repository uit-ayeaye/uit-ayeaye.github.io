Hledan Junction

Kamayut Township, Yangon

The crossing at the heart of Yangon's university quarter, rebuilt as a real-time map. This is the same geometry that runs in the mobile build — re-exported, cleaned up, and pushed straight to WebGL.

If you know Hledan, you know it by the shape of an ordinary evening: buses stacking up under the flyover, tea-shop stools out on the pavement by four, the bookstalls, and a crossing everybody learns to time. Half the people who grew up on that corner are somewhere else now. This is here so you can walk it again from wherever that is — set it to golden hour and it is about right.

The original export carried twelve cameras, nine lights and five ground planes stacked twelve millimetres apart. All of it is gone here; what is left is the city.

Play drops a Straw Hat onto the street. Movement, kit and cooldowns are the same model as the Elbaf build — 5.6 m/s, sprint at 1.75x, gravity −18, coyote time, one air jump — with no physics engine anywhere in it.

Ground height comes from a 171 KB PNG; what you can walk into comes from the map's own triangles, sorted at load into walls and floors. That is why you can walk under the flyover rather than on top of it, and why the pavements under the trees are open: a height field with one layer per cell cannot hold a road beneath a bridge, and the canopy overhead was closing streets you could plainly see were empty.

The map is not in metres: measured against the parked buses, whose length and height agree independently, it runs about 1.5x life size — so the character and every one of its motion constants are scaled by the same factor. That keeps the proportions honest without re-baking anything.

Controls — Elbaf's, key for key: WASD move, Space jump (twice in the air), Shift sprint, Q strike, hold F for the sustained move, R heavy, E rocket / flash, T the big one, G Gear Second, hold V Balloon, H Haki, C roll, Tab swap. On a phone: left thumb steers, right thumb looks, tap to strike, hold for the sustained version.
