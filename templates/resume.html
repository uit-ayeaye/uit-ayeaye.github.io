The person behind the work

I’m Thomas D. Lynn, also known as Thiha Lynn: an independent software engineer, creative developer, and founder of Backbenchers Studio. With roots in Myanmar, my work connects practical software with games, culture, learning, and the web.

I build across the whole product: the screen a visitor sees, the app a customer carries, the dashboard a team operates, and the services that connect them. My portfolio spans multiplayer games, logistics, commerce, publishing, education, creative tools, and browser worlds.

A little pirate spirit runs through this logbook. For me, that means curiosity, an independent approach, and making room for people’s stories alongside the engineering. The playful presentation comes with real project notes, clear contributions, and credit for the people whose work helped shape each build.

How I approach a build

  • Start with the people using it. A merchant’s order flow, a courier’s handover, a teacher’s portfolio, and a player’s first session all need different decisions.
  • Connect the whole experience. I work across public interfaces, mobile clients, APIs, administrative tools, and the workflows behind them.
  • Make character useful. Typography, motion, and visual storytelling should help people understand what they can do next.
  • Design for the device in hand. Responsive layouts, touch controls, reduced-motion settings, offline features, and lower-powered-device modes appear throughout the work.
  • Keep the story honest. I distinguish original work from adaptations, label demonstrations, protect internal data, and preserve creator credits.

Hledan is especially personal: my friend Madric A made the original map of this little piece of Yangon. My contribution brings it to the browser through lighting, weather, navigation, and performance work. His authorship remains part of the experience.
