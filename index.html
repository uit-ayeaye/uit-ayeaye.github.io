ONE CAPTAIN.
A lot of hats.

I’m Thomas, also known as Thiha Lynn. Software engineer. Captain of Backbenchers Studio. A builder with Myanmar in his story.

I move between games, web platforms, mobile apps, and the less glamorous systems that make them work: admin tools, integrations, publishing pipelines, and order workflows.

Some of this work is for businesses. Some is for a community. Some is for a childhood friend, a poet, a teacher, or a band. The common thread is making something that feels like it belongs to the people using it.

The One Piece spirit? Freedom to explore.
And remembering who’s on your crew.
