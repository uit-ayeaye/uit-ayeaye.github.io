VOYAGE 04 / Platforms

Live publication

Myo Taw News

A publishing backbone for Myanmar affairs, local reporting, and the stories around Nay Pyi Taw.

Independent media · Myanmar

THE STORY

Why this voyage matters.

Myo Taw needs to publish quickly, keep Burmese typography readable, and bring articles and video together. The project supports a newsroom covering current Myanmar affairs and conflict, with attention to Nay Pyi Taw and local communities.

MY CONTRIBUTION

What I brought aboard.

Backend and implementation for the news platform, content-management workflows, and public reading experience.

INSIDE THE BUILD

The details that make it work.

  • Article and video publishing with categories and a dedicated editorial management surface.
  • Local Laravel/Filament implementation with media import and publishing services.
  • Burmese, English, and Chinese navigation is available on the public site.
  • Tools for video ingestion, translation, and social content organization are present in the source.
