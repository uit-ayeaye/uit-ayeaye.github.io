PROJECT 03 / Commerce

Android available · iOS pending

BurmaMart

One marketplace connecting buyers, merchants, and cross-border operations across Myanmar, Thailand, and Singapore.

MSCT PTE LTD · Singapore

THE STORY

The context.

Buying across borders involves more than a product card. BurmaMart brings local currencies, delivery routes, merchants, and the people handling parcels into one system. It gives shoppers a familiar buying experience while giving sellers their own mobile workspace.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My role.

Implementation across the storefront, shared backend, administration, buyer app, and merchant app for MSCT PTE LTD.

INSIDE THE BUILD

The details that make it work.

  • Buyer and merchant Flutter apps share a Laravel API, MySQL data model, and Filament administration.
  • Localized product discovery, carts, orders, seller catalog management, and buyer–merchant conversations.
  • Multi-currency commerce and country-aware fulfillment, including office handover workflows.
  • Android availability and pending iOS release are recorded as supplied by the project owner; both app codebases are present.

TECHNOLOGIES

  • Laravel
  • Filament
  • Flutter
  • MySQL
