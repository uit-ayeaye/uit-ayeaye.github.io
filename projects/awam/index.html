PROJECT 02 / Platforms

Web + mobile + bots

Actually We ARe MAd

Myanmar meme culture, from a swipeable reel to a whole creator and digital-store ecosystem.

Domain LAB LTD · Myanmar

THE STORY

The context.

AWAM starts with a very local kind of internet humor: Burmese dialogue, reaction footage, movie edits, and the community that remixes them. The work grew beyond a meme feed into a place to discover creators, find footage, and connect entertainment with merchandise and affordable digital products.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My role.

Full-stack platform and ecosystem implementation: the public experience, media processing, administration, mobile surfaces, and Telegram workflows.

INSIDE THE BUILD

The details that make it work.

  • Vertical reels, discovery, creator profiles, and a searchable meme-footage culture.
  • Next.js application with Prisma/PostgreSQL and FFmpeg processing for adaptive HLS playback.
  • Native Android and iOS project surfaces alongside web and creator tools.
  • AWAM merchandise and the @AWAM_Digital_Store_bot ecosystem, with Telegram-driven administration and approval workflows.

COLLABORATORS / CREDITS
