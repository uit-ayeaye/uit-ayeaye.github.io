VOYAGE 10 / People & Culture

Ongoing project

EXTANT

A digital stage for a Myanmar metal band: releases, performances, and an identity that hits hard.

Myanmar · djent & metalcore

THE STORY

Why this voyage matters.

EXTANT needs a home that carries the energy of the band while making the actual work easy to find. The current public experience organizes releases, performance imagery, member credits, and a history of the band around a deliberately uncompromising visual identity.

MY CONTRIBUTION

What I brought aboard.

Ongoing band-site design and implementation, shaping music, imagery, release information, and contact into one immersive experience.

INSIDE THE BUILD

The details that make it work.

  • Release-focused presentation with dedicated music and video sections.
  • Band member credits and a timeline sit alongside the discography.
  • Live performance imagery and an editorial, music-first visual approach.
  • Responsive presentation and direct paths to the band’s published work.
