PROJECT 20 / Immersive

Playable fan project

Elbaf: The Under-Realm

Under the roots of a giant tree, a chained Loki is waiting to be found.

One Piece · fan-made experiment

THE STORY

The context.

A fan-made One Piece action prototype set beneath Elbaf. The experience is built around exploration: a dark under-realm, playable fighters, a key sealed in ice, and seastone wards between the player and a chained giant.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My role.

Interactive browser experience, scene composition, procedural character work, and keyboard/touch interaction.

INSIDE THE BUILD

The details that make it work.

  • Third-person exploration with a selectable Straw Hat crew.
  • Hand-authored poses over generated rigs.
  • A small environmental quest around Loki, a key, and five wards.
  • React Three Fiber and Rapier with keyboard and touch controls.

COLLABORATORS / CREDITS

Unofficial fan work inspired by One Piece. Characters and the original world belong to their respective rights holders.

TECHNOLOGIES

  • Procedural rigging
