PROJECT 05 / Platforms

Live web platform

STRIKERS

Football scores, match details, and editorial stories in English and Myanmar.

Football · English & Myanmar

THE STORY

The context.

STRIKERS brings fixtures, results, football news, and guides into a bilingual matchday experience. The public portal connects a Laravel publishing dashboard with a cached football data integration, making score freshness and available coverage visible to readers.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My role.

Football portal design and full-stack implementation, including responsive match browsing, bilingual content, editorial administration, and the football-data integration.

INSIDE THE BUILD

The details that make it work.

  • Date and competition filters, match details, search, and device-local favourites.
  • English and Myanmar reading experiences with dedicated article pages.
  • Cached provider data with visible freshness indicators and request-budget controls.
  • Editorial management for news, guides, promotions, and broadcast listings.

COLLABORATORS / CREDITS

Match data is supplied by API-Football. Third-party reporting, team marks, and broadcast media belong to their respective creators.
