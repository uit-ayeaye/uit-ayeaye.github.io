SOM BI

Turning fragmented academic performance records into a shared picture for a university school.

MFU · School of Management

SCHOOL OF MANAGEMENTSOMBI

THE STORY

Why this voyage matters.

Built as a senior project for the School of Management at Mae Fah Luang University, SOM BI addresses an administrative problem: individual lecturer records are difficult to understand at program level. The project brings those records into role-based dashboards and visual comparisons for the school.

MY CONTRIBUTION

What I brought aboard.

Team contribution to implementation, integration, internal setup, and deployment as part of the Vanguard senior-project team.

INSIDE THE BUILD

The details that make it work.

  • Nuxt/Vue dashboards for administrators and lecturers, backed by Express and MySQL.
  • Integration with the university E-port KPI system across teaching, research, academic service, administration, and arts and culture.
  • Program mapping, evaluation-period selection, aggregate views, and individual drill-downs.
  • Institution-scoped authentication and role-based access. The operational deployment and university data remain private.

THE CREW / CREDITS

A Vanguard team senior project for Mae Fah Luang University’s School of Management. Presented here as a public case study only; no internal dashboard or staff data is published.
