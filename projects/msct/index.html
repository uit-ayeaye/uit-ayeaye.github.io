PROJECT 18 / Commerce

Company website

MSCT PTE LTD

A corporate home for the import and distribution business behind BurmaMart.

Singapore · BurmaMart parent company

THE STORY

The context.

MSCT’s website tells a different part of the commerce story: the company behind the products and the marketplace. It introduces the Singapore-based import and distribution business, its food and everyday-goods categories, and how sourcing connects to delivery.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My role.

Corporate website implementation and the public connection between MSCT’s business and the BurmaMart app ecosystem.

INSIDE THE BUILD

The details that make it work.

  • Service and sourcing information for retail and business partners.
  • A clear source-to-shelf explanation of the company’s work.
  • BurmaMart app presentation with real product and browsing screens.
  • Business contact and company identity in a responsive static site.
