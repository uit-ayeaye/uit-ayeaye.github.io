PROJECT 01 / Games

Relaunch in preparation

End Game Union

A multiplayer game with a story beyond the screen. Built in Myanmar, carried by a community, and still being written.

Backbenchers Studio · lifelong project

THE STORY

The context.

My lifelong project. End Game Union grew from Myanmar’s Spring Revolution into a multiplayer experience made with fellow Burmese developers. It connects my love of games with the people and place I come from. Contemporary reporting documented its early announcement in 2021 and its rise to the top of Myanmar’s local Play Store chart in July 2022. That history belongs to the whole team and community.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My role.

Game development and the wider End Game Union ecosystem with Backbenchers Studio and fellow developers. The next chapter, Reloaded, brings the multiplayer game, progression, supporting web experience, and music together.

INSIDE THE BUILD

The details that make it work.

  • Mobile multiplayer FPS with Photon networking and touch controls.
  • Reloaded introduces runner and match-3 experiences alongside multiplayer progression and customization.
  • An accompanying web experience brings together existing store listings, gameplay, and the Reloaded soundtrack by Double U (DJ Wine) × Backbenchers Studio.
  • Existing Android and iOS releases; the official site currently labels the new relaunch and payment services as not yet open.

COLLABORATORS / CREDITS

Created with Daniel Kong Thar and the End Game Union team. Published historically through Shoot and Support. Soundtrack: Double U (DJ Wine) × Backbenchers Studio.
