VOYAGE 13 / People & Culture

Portfolio

Hein Zaw Fitness

A strong, movement-led portfolio for a Yangon fitness coach.

Certified trainer · Yangon

THE STORY

Why this voyage matters.

This portfolio introduces Hein Zaw as a certified personal trainer and body-transformation coach. The work brings training imagery, qualifications, services, and a clear personal identity into a focused, mobile-friendly presentation.

MY CONTRIBUTION

What I brought aboard.

Fitness portfolio design and frontend implementation, with scroll-linked motion and reusable content sections.

INSIDE THE BUILD

The details that make it work.

  • React/Vite frontend built as a static site.
  • Framer Motion and Lenis support the movement-led presentation.
  • Training imagery, qualifications, and service information form the core story.
  • Responsive layouts make the portfolio usable while browsing on a phone.
