PROJECT 24 / Immersive

Adaptation & extension

Grand Line Fizz

A fictional soda brand, a crew of character cans, and a scroll-powered voyage.

One Piece · 3D can experience

THE STORY

The context.

A 3D product-storytelling experiment that turns One Piece character artwork into a fictional drinks collection. Floating cans, scene transitions, the crew carousel, and an ocean scene connect the product presentation to a playful fan world.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My role.

Adaptation and extension of the credited 3D base project: character cans, content, scene work, interaction, and static deployment.

INSIDE THE BUILD

The details that make it work.

  • GLTF can rendering with character-label textures and HDR lighting.
  • GSAP-linked transitions and camera movement.
  • Fourteen character cans, a carousel, and a ship/ocean scene in the editable source.
  • Next.js static export for the existing GitHub Pages route.
