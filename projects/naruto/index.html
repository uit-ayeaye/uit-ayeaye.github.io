PROJECT 25 / Immersive

Adaptation & optimization

Sage Mode Awakening

A scroll-driven transformation with lightning, chakra, and a cinematic sense of timing.

Naruto · scroll experiment

THE STORY

The context.

An anime interaction study that uses the scroll position as a timeline. Base form, hand seal, and Sage Mode become three stages of a single transformation, with graphic effects reinforcing the progression.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My role.

Integration, presentation, and immersive-web adaptation of a credited original showcase.

INSIDE THE BUILD

The details that make it work.

  • Pinned transformation sequence driven by GSAP ScrollTrigger.
  • Canvas lightning, rising particles, and camera-shake effects.
  • A focused study in sequencing motion to user input.
