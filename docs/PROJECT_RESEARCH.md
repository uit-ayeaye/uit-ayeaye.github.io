## Method and scope

Reviewed the supplied project list, the Backbenchers logo presentation, the supplied cyber Jolly Roger, accessible public websites, and matching local project documentation/source. Public website screenshots were captured in a fresh browser session. No internal university data, production credentials, customer records, or private admin screenshots were copied into the portfolio.

The first-person role descriptions come from Thomas’s supplied brief. Source inspection supports the stated technologies and capabilities; it is not an independent verification of sole authorship, every production feature, or app-store approval. Collaborative and adapted work retains explicit credits. No employment dates, degrees, commercial revenue, donation totals, or performance benchmarks have been invented.

## End Game Union: source trail

- [Official site and current relaunch status](https://endgameunion.com/) — identifies the multiplayer game, runner and match-3 features, current relaunch preparation, and Double U × Backbenchers soundtrack. The new services/payments are not described as open.
- [Existing Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.endgameunion.endgame) — public Android release record. Its download counter is not used as a portfolio KPI.
- [Existing App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/mm/app/end-game-union-multiplayer/id1626728756) — existing iOS product and Reloaded-related description.
- [DVB, 20 November 2021](https://burmese.dvb.no/post/501525) — early project announcement. The portfolio calls this an early beginning rather than asserting the same release date for every platform.
- [DVB, 28 July 2022](https://burmese.dvb.no/post/545683) — contemporary Myanmar local Play Store chart reporting; identifies Thomas in the report.
- [Mizzima, 28 July 2022](https://www.mizzima.com/article/spring-revolution-daily-news-28-july-2022) — additional contemporary reporting of the chart milestone, attributed to the developers.
- [Myanmar Now, 23 September 2022](https://myanmar-now.org/en/news/myanmar-junta-bans-online-pdf-video-games/) — historical context of the regime’s ban, covering several games including End Game Union.
- [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/End_Game:_Union_Multiplayer) — discovery of background/reference links. The article contains editorial quality notices and conflicting release framing; fundraising totals and “first ever” claims were not reused.

Reports about **War of Heroes / The PDF Game** were not treated as End Game Union statistics. The portfolio uses a short original historical summary and links out to coverage rather than reproducing articles.
